# Wins And Losses {#two-attributes}

*This chapter has not been written yet.*

Options described by two attributes: a chance of winning and a chance of losing. The
drift weights the two differences separately ($v = \beta_{win}\,\Delta p_{win} -
\beta_{loss}\,\Delta p_{loss}$), so the model can say whether wins or losses weigh more.
The boundary can then depend on how much is at stake on each trial, which asks whether
people become more cautious when more is on the line.
